## 1. Market Structure: European Data Center Capacity

### Market Size & Growth (2024–2026)

Europe data center market valued at USD 59.84 billion in 2025, forecast USD 148.82 billion by 2031 (16.40% CAGR). Europe data center colocation market: **USD 11.65 billion in 2025, projected USD 46.51 billion by 2031 (25.95% CAGR).**
**Source:** https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/11/3360148/28124/en/europe-data-center-colocation-market-forecast-to-reach-usd-46-51-billion-by-2031-as-ai-and-cloud-demand-accelerate.html (Sep 2026)

### Investment Breakdown: Colocation vs. Self-Build

**Colocation dominates** with 68% of European data center construction investment in 2025; hyperscale self-build records strongest 19.21% CAGR. **Colocation services: 63.05% of market by type; hyperscale self-build expands fastest.**
**Source:** Same GlobeNewswire report (Sep 2026)

### Hyperscaler & Major Developer Pipeline

**EMEA hyperscale self-build pipeline:** 742 MW under construction + 2,661 MW land-banked.
**Source:** JLL (from WebSearch result, 2024–2025)

**Top EU developers by capacity:** Equinix, Digital Realty, NTT Global Data Centers, Telehouse KDDI, Global Switch account for 40–45% regional market share.
**Source:** ResearchAndMarkets via BusinessWire (Jan 2026)

**Major investor-backed platforms:**
- **KKR & Global Technical Realty (GTR):** KKR committed additional $1.5B to GTR European platform; GTR developing campuses UK, France, Spain, Israel.
- **Blackstone:** $70B data center investment plan (announced late 2023); hyperscale campuses across U.S. and Europe.
- **Brookfield:** Acquired Data4 (EUR ~3.8B, April 2023); expanded European portfolio.
- **DigitalBridge:** 5 platforms (U.S., APAC, EMEA); 155+ facilities, 15 GW development pipeline.

**Source:** https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260107040486/en/KKR-and-Oak-Hill-Capital-Commit-Nearly-%242-Billion-to-Leading-European-Data-Center-Platform-Global-Technical-Realty (Jan 2026) & DataCenterMagazine top 10 investors

### Regional Growth: FLAP-D vs. Secondary/Emerging Markets

**FLAP-D (Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Dublin) status:** Traditional leaders face severe grid and land constraints; remain high demand due to hyperscaler pressure. Average interconnection queue time: **7–10 years in legacy hubs.**
**Source:** Multiple fetches confirm grid congestion (IEA, Ember reports); interconnection delays documented in Ashurst, DLA Piper analyses

**Secondary & Emerging Markets Growth:**
- **Nordics & Southern Europe:** Expected to grow 30–55% annually (vs. FLAP-D slowdown).
- **Nordic expansion driver:** Available renewable energy, grid headroom.
- **Southern Europe expansion:** Intercontinental subsea cable access (Iberia, Mediterranean).
- **Colocation in secondary markets:** UK (excluding London) growing 28.84% CAGR.

**Source:** DCD/DataCenterDynamics analyses + Cushman & Wakefield EMEA reports

## KEY FINDINGS (Executive Summary)

1. **Market Structure:** European colocation dominates (68% capex); 11.65 → 46.51 billion USD by 2031. Hyperscale self-build fastest-growing (19.21% CAGR). Investor-backed platforms (KKR/GTR, Blackstone, Brookfield, DigitalBridge) consolidating European capacity.

2. **FLAP-D Gridlock:** Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Dublin face 7–10 year interconnection queues; secondary markets (Nordics, Southern Europe) growing 30–55% annually as grid-constrained sites saturate.

3. **Advisory Landscape:** CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, Arup, Turner & Townsend, Knight Frank, Savills all offer site selection, due diligence, and capital markets advisory. No public pricing; engagements appear fully custom.

4. **Software Competitors (Direct):** Enverus, LandGate, Paces, Nira Energy, Transect dominate specialized siting/grid analysis; DatacenterHawk, DC Byte, Baxtel, 451 Research, Structure Research, TeleGeography provide market intelligence/facility databases. No pricing disclosed; all subscription/access-based.

5. **Grid Capacity Data:** Dutch Capaciteitskaart (weekly updates, live), UK DNO heat maps (live via Power Networks), BNetzA (Germany, regulatory reform stage). TSO/DSO transparency improving but still a bottleneck; highest-demand regions show 3,600+ MW waiting lists.

6. **Direct Competitors Identified:** Enverus (grid/parcel screening), LandGate (offtake capacity + land marketplace), Paces (AI-driven siting + interconnection services), Nira Energy (transmission capacity POI identification), Transect (environmental due diligence).
