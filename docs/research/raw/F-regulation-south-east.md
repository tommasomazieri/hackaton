# Data Center Regulations in Southern & Central/Eastern Europe: Grid Access, Siting & Water Restrictions

**Research Date: September 26, 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

This desk research identifies the national and regional rules, grid-access procedures, moratoria, permitting requirements, and water restrictions that block, delay, or shape where new large data centers (≥10 MW) can be built across southern and central/eastern Europe as of September 2026.

**Key finding**: Grid access capacity is the primary binding constraint across the region. Aragón (Spain) water scarcity and permitting fragmentation present secondary barriers. Portugal and Italy have undertaken regulatory streamlining (2026), but Italy's grid backlog (50+ GW) remains acute. Poland's UC-84 reform (March 2026) raises capital requirements. No hard moratoria identified, but de facto barriers exist through grid allocation mechanisms and financial guarantees.

## Summary of Barriers by Type

### Grid Access & Capacity Allocation
- **Spain**: CNMC permits (Aug. 2026) introduce flexible access but maintain scarcity; demand tenders favor renewables over data centers.
- **Italy**: LD 21/2026 streamlines permitting, but 50+ GW backlog at Terna remains binding constraint.
- **Poland**: UC-84 reform (Mar. 2026) raises financial barriers; PSE allocates 1,200 MW by 2034 (constrained).
- **Austria**: 200 MW minimum capacity threshold excludes smaller projects from transmission grid.
- **Greece**: ADMIE capacity limited; 284 MVA expected requests by 2030.
- **Portugal**: National Data Center Plan (Apr. 2026) designed to reduce barriers; grid pre-zoned.

### Water Restrictions
- **Spain (Aragón)**: Regional water concession required; announced projects require ~10 million m³/year in semi-arid region (320 l/m² rainfall). Critical constraint.
- **Other countries**: Not identified as binding.

### Permitting & Environmental Clearance
- **Spain**: Fragmented regional/local requirements; no national homogenous standard.
- **Czech Republic**: Lengthy permitting (1–2 year construction but permitting timeline not specified) remains bottleneck despite €3.3B grid investment.
- **Italy**: LD 21/2026 unified authorization (10–13 months) streamlines multi-agency approvals.
- **Portugal**: PNCD (Apr. 2026) sets maximum licensing deadlines (specifics not quantified in sources).

### Foreign Investment & Security Screening
- **Greece**: FDI Law 5202/2025 introduces national security screening for data center projects.

### Financial / Capital Requirements
- **Poland**: Advance payment doubled PLN 30 → 60/kW; cap PLN 3M → 6M (UC-84, Mar. 2026).
- **Romania**: Financial guarantee 5% → 20% of grid tariff; EUR 20,000/MW deposit required for auctions (ANRE Orders 15/16, May 2026).

## Moratoria Status

**No hard moratoria identified** as of September 2026 in the countries surveyed. De facto constraints exist through:
- Grid allocation mechanisms (Spain CNMC, Italy Terna, Poland PSE, Austria APG minimum capacity)
- Financial guarantees (Poland, Romania)
- Water concession requirements (Spain/Aragón)
- FDI screening (Greece)
